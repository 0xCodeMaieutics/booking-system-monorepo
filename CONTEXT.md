# Face Aesthetic Centre

The online presence and, later, the appointment booking for Face Aesthetic Centre, an aesthetic medicine clinic in Kutaisi, Georgia.

## Language

**Clinic**:
Face Aesthetic Centre (ესთეტიკის ცენტრი ფეისი) — the business and physical location at Tamar Mepe #38, Kutaisi. The Clinic is the public-facing brand.
_Avoid_: Salon, studio, centre (on its own), practice

**Practitioner**:
A qualified person who performs treatments at the Clinic. Dr. Khatuna Robakidze is the lead Practitioner.
_Avoid_: Doctor (as a role name), specialist, staff, provider

**Patient**:
A person who receives, or wants to receive, Treatments at the Clinic.
_Avoid_: Client, customer, user

**Treatment**:
A named aesthetic procedure the Clinic offers to patients, such as dermal fillers or a chemical peel.
_Avoid_: Service, procedure, product

**Landing Page**:
The Clinic's public website that introduces the Clinic and its Practitioners and directs visitors to contact or visit.
_Avoid_: Homepage, site, website (when the landing page specifically is meant)

**Locale**:
One of the languages the Clinic's public content is published in: Georgian (`ka`) or English (`en`).
_Avoid_: Translation, language version
